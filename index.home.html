Our Story

A Private World
Above the Skyline

ALTURA was born from a singular vision — to create a space where the city's most discerning individuals could gather in absolute privacy, surrounded by curated beauty and impeccable service.

Perched 42 floors above the urban pulse, our rooftop lounge offers an atmosphere that blends raw architecture with warmth and exclusivity. Every detail has been considered. Every evening, crafted.
